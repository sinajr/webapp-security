This is the attacker's page in demo 4 of the web security lab. It just tried to change the email address of whoever is logged into the forum at 127.0.0.1:8000. Go and reload http://127.0.0.1:8000/profile/ to see whether it worked.

In vulnerable mode it will have worked. In SECURE_MODE=1 the forum answers 403 Forbidden and the email is unchanged. To see the 403 yourself, remove target="hidden-frame" from the form so the response is displayed instead of hidden.
